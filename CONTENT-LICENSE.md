The content in `data/` is covered as follows.

| File | What it is | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data/lessons.json`, lessons with `"n": "pc-NN"` | Lessons adapted from the Peace Corps handbook *Gagana Samoa mo Pisikoa* (Peace Corps, 1991). The handbook material is public domain (a work of the United States government); the practice items and other additions written for this project are CC BY-SA 4.0. | Public domain + CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `data/lessons.json`, lessons with `"n": "g-..."` | Lessons, explanations, examples, dialogues and practice written for this project. A few phrases in them come from J. F. Mayer's Peace Corps course *Samoan Language* (1975, public domain), as their `sources` say. | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `data/journey.json` | The Malaga journey: stories, characters and talks written for this project. The people are made up. | CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| `data/course.json` | The map of worlds and the order of the lessons | CC BY-SA 4.0 |

| `data/pacific.json` | Coastlines from Natural Earth (1:50m) | Public domain |
